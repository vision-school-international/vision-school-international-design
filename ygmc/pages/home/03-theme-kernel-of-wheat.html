YGMC Theme: Kernel of Wheat

Truly, truly, I say to you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains alone; but if it dies, it bears much fruit.

JOHN 12:24

Throughout history, God has always anointed His young generation to advance His kingdom to the final frontiers.

In 1886, we saw the greatest mission revival in the world - over 20,000 North American college students offering their lives in the prime of their youth as kernels of wheat for the darkest corners of the world. Through them, the destiny of millions of people and countless nations completely changed.

We believe that in these days, God is raising up his Final Student Volunteer Movement. In these Last Days, He is raising up a holy generation that will Finish the Task of the Great Commission. There is great calling on our generation; May we rise up as true disciples that will not love our lives even unto the point of death and live for His kingdom alone!
