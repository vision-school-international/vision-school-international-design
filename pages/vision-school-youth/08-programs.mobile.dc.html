Youth Programs

YVS is a requirement to participate in Field Operation (2 to 3-week mission trip) and High School Student Missions (2-month mission trip).

UBTJ

Youth Back to Jesus

UBTJ is a global youth mission prayer movement; members worship, pray, and evangelize in their middle schools and high schools. The hope for UBTJ is to bring prayer and worship revival upon the youth generation in North America and in all nations. Currently, there are UBTJ in several countries, including Korea, Australia, Japan, and India.

MA:RCH

Mission America: Reveal Christ’s Heart

Every winter, there is a 3 to 5-day outreach program called MA:RCH, during which youth from North America unite with other youth in their regions to see their generation rise up for mission revival. MA:RCH is mainly composed of city ministry, school ministry, and vision nights intended to both evangelize to non-believers and awaken believers to God’s global vision to see all the nations worship Jesus Christ.

HSM

High School Student Missions

HSM is a 2-month long summer mission trip to a country in the 10/40 Window. In teams led by adult supervisors, youth go out to partake in frontier ministry in the unreached nations. Through training in the Word, worship, prayer, and evangelism, HSM prepares youth to be spiritual leaders that will stir up gospel revival in the nations!
