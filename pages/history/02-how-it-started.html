How It All Started

From a Few Students to a Global Mission

InterCP was founded in 1983 by a small group of university students who had given their lives to pioneering frontier mission.

It began in August 1983 as Korea Tentmaking Mission, a name that declared its vision of pioneer mission. Its Mission Spirit was Frontier Mission, Discipleship, Field Ministry, Women Ministry, and Partnership.

On 29 September 1983, the first missionary couple was sent to Turkey, and with that, the overseas mission work of InterCP officially began.

In 1992 the mission was reorganized and renamed InterCP, and it recommitted itself to the last unreached peoples.

Its focus became the Islamic and Buddhist worlds and the minority peoples of Eurasia. By 2026 it had sent about 5,900 missionaries, with about 70 branches in Korea and 400 overseas.
