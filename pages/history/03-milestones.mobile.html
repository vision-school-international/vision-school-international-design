The Story So Far

Milestones in Mission

1974 —

The Lausanne Covenant is signed. It later becomes the base of our statement of faith.

1983 —

Founded in August by a small group of university students.

1983 —

First missionary couple sent to Turkey on 29 September.

1992 —

Renamed InterCP and recommitted to the unreached peoples.

2011 —

Mission statement renewed on 9 June 2011.

2015 —

North America MnM ministry begins.

2026 —

Mission Camp’s 19th year.

2026 —

About 5,900 missionaries sent to 55+ peoples.

Today —

Over 4,500 staff serving in branches around the world.
