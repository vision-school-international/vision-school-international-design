Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Being an SM?

Who can become a Student Missionary?

Current undergraduate college students over 18 years old who have graduated from Vision School and served six months in InterCP International campus ministry.

How long is the commission?

SMs serve eight months to one year on the field in a team, after one month of training at InterCP International Headquarters.

When are SMs sent out to the field?

There are two dispatches each year: summer in June and July, and winter in December and January.

Do SMs go alone?

No. SMs go in team to the unreached people groups for one year to plant churches.

Is an SM different from a YTM?

SMs are current undergraduate college students over 18; YTMs are young adults under 35. Both go in teams for one year.

What happens after the year?

SMs keep applying their training, leading campus ministries and returning to the field for short-term church-planting mission trips.

What legacy do Student Missionaries carry forward?

Since the 1806 Haystack Prayer Movement, God has used students for world evangelization. From the 1886 Mount Hermon Conference came the Student Volunteer Movement: 100,000 young people committed their lives, and 20,500 set sail.

Who do I contact with questions?

Email campus@intercp.org and ask about the next Student Missionary dispatch.
