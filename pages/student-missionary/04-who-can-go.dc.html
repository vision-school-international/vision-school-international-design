Who can become a Student Missionary?

Undergraduates over 18 who have graduated from Vision School and served six months in InterCP campus ministry.

College Students

Current undergraduate students over 18 years old.

Trained

Vision School graduates, plus a month of training at InterCP HQ.

Six Months Served

Six months of InterCP International Campus ministry.

Commissioning Schedule

Summer dispatch: June–July. Winter dispatch: December–January.
