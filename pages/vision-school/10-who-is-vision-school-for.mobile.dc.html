Who Is Vision School
Made For?

There is a Vision School for every generation, including young adults and college students, each with its own schedule and team.

Everyone

Anyone who is interested in and/or has desire to discover God’s heart and vision for this world. It consists of worship, lectures, prayer, small groups, and fellowship.

Learn More

Youth & Children

Youth Vision School is a 9-week program for middle school and high school students, and Kids Vision School is an 8-week program that engages children in grades 1 to 6 through worship, lectures, media clips, small groups, and activities and games.

Learn More

Pastors & Seniors

Pastors, church leaders, and adults over 40 have their own Vision School, as do women, working women, and foreign residents in Korea, so every generation can discover God’s heart and vision.
