Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Mission Impact Teams?

What does a Mission Impact Team do?

MITs visit the unreached nations to minister in various ways, including kids’ ministry, women’s ministry, men’s ministry, art, worship and intercessory prayer.

Do I need to finish Vision School first?

No. This specific team doesn’t require Vision School graduates.

When do teams go?

Campus and kids teams go during the summer and winter breaks.

Can university students join?

Yes. University students go together to regions where unreached peoples live, serving through peace volunteering and academic and cultural exchange between universities.

Can kids join?

Yes. After Kids Vision School, children go every summer and winter for evangelization, city ministry, English camps, homestay and church ministry.

Is MIT part of a larger ministry?

Yes. The adult MIT is part of All Nations Adults (ANA), alongside ANA Vision School, ANA Field Operation and ANA GAP Prayer.

What is the vision of All Nations Adults?

To share God’s vision with ethnic churches in North America and their native nations, raising diverse ethnic churches to go together and preach the gospel to the ends of the earth.

Does MIT include worship ministry?

Yes. MIT is listed among InterCP’s professional ministries as its praise and worship ministry.

Who do I contact with questions?

Email northamerica@intercp.org and ask about the next Mission Impact Team.
