Who can become a Young Tent-Maker?

Young adults under 35 who have graduated from Vision School and served six months in InterCP young adult ministry.

Young Adults

Open to young adults under 35 years old, sent out in teams.

Trained

Vision School graduates, plus a month of training at InterCP HQ.

Six Months Served

Six months of InterCP International Young Adult ministry.

Longer Commitments

Also Young Generation Missionaries (2-year) and Short and Long-Term Missionaries (2-5 year).
