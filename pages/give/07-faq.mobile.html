Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?
We’ve Got Answers.

How can I partner with InterCP?

Through prayer, financial support, and volunteering. Each one helps prepare the way of the Lord among the nations.

What can my giving support?

Field missionaries, domestic ministry staff, project ministries, and all other mission work of InterCP International, at home and abroad.

How do I start giving?

Call or text our giving office at 070-8787-8863~4, and our team will guide you through the detailed ways you can give to the mission work.

Give to a missionary?

Yes. Each missionary has their own support account, so please contact the giving office before you give, so that your gift reaches the right missionary.

Can I give without naming a purpose or a missionary?

No. We receive designated gifts only, so please name the purpose or the missionary you support.

What about tithes?

We teach that tithes belong to your own local church. Gifts to InterCP are designated offerings for specific mission work.

Can I get a receipt for my donation?

Yes. Donation receipts are available. Contact the giving office to request one, or to ask for prayer letters, prayer cards, or a change of address.

How much should a sending church give?

We recommend the sending church cover about a third of the budget; our network supplies the rest.

Why does missionary support differ by country and year?

Too much support can harm a missionary’s work, so support is set by country and year.
