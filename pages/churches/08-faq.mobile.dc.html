Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?
We’ve Got Answers.

Who is the subject of world mission?

We believe it is the local church. Mission organizations support it through mobilization, education, and cooperation.

Are all missionaries church-sent?

Yes. Every InterCP missionary, without exception, is sent by a local church. We do not recognize anyone the church has not approved and sent.

Where does training happen?

Wherever possible, all of our training is held within the local church and in cooperation with it, so trainees grow inside their own church.

What about tithes?

We teach and guide our members to give their tithes to their own local church, and we encourage every trainee to serve that church faithfully and with maturity.

How do members prepare to go out as field missionaries?

They consult their senior pastor or mission elder so training proceeds in order and maturity.

Any gift rules?

We receive designated gifts only. Please name the specific purpose or missionary; undesignated gifts are not accepted.

What share should a sending church give?

We recommend that a church support about a third of its missionary’s budget. Our network at home and abroad supplies the rest, so support is not excessive.

Can InterCP present at our church?

Yes. Our regional staff can present Mission Camp at your church and set up an informational booth.

What if a member is not serving our church in a good way?

Please let us know, and we will guide them to serve your local church with maturity.
