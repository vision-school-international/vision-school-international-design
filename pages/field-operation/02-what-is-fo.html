What is a Field Operation (FO)?

Each summer and winter, Christians from all around the world step out in faith to take the gospel of the kingdom to the people and the places where the gospel has never been preached before. FO is unlike any other short-term mission trip: you witness the Great Commission with your own eyes.

Two to Three Weeks

Every summer and winter, teams spend two to three weeks in an unreached nation.

Going in Faith

Trusting in the Lord and guided by the Holy Spirit, teams go where no Christian has set foot in thousands of years.

Language and Culture

You learn the language and culture of the people group you serve, eating, crying and praying with them.

The 10/40 Window

FO goes to the unreached nations of the 10/40 Window, to people who have never had the opportunity to hear the truth of Jesus Christ.

Vision School Graduates

The only requirement to join a Field Operation is completing Vision School.

Shaped by Each Nation

The specifics of each FO depend on the spiritual condition and field realities of each nation.
