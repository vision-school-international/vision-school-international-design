Short-Term Trip

FO is unlike any other short-term mission trip. You receive a deeper understanding of who God is and His desire for all tribes, tongues and peoples to return to Him, and take part in God’s history.

Preach the Good News

Go and share the good news where it has never been preached.

Live With the People

See God at Work

Witness the fulfillment of the Great Commission with your own eyes.
