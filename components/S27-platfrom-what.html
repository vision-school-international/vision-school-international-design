What is Vision School Platform?

Vision School Platform gives ministries and developers access to the same trusted technology behind the Bible App. With a range of APIs and SDKs, you can integrate Scripture into your apps and websites, easily access licensed bible versions, and build meaningful experiences faster, for more people.

Fast-Track Bible Licensing

Easily access licensed Bible versions from Vision School’s approved Bible Publishing Partners.

Data and Insights

Understand Scripture engagement, track performance, and make informed decisions using analytics.

Multi-Platform Support

Build once, deploy anywhere. Use SDKs for Web, React Native, Swift, and Kotlin to reach users across mobile and web.

Bible SDK

Add Vision School’s Bible reader to your digital products. Choose from hundreds of trusted translations and reduce development time with ready-to-use components.

Sign In With Vision School

Let users connect with your products using their Vision School account.

Developer Dashboard

Manage all your integrations, monitor usage, and access support from a single, simplified dashboard.
