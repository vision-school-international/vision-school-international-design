What is a Student Missionary (SM)?

Student Missionaries (SMs) are college students who go in teams to the unreached people groups for one year to plant churches, dreaming God’s dream for rapid evangelization of the world. They make disciples through incarnation mission and train to become leaders of the global mission movement.

Campus Church Planting

SMs dream of rapid world evangelization through church planting on unreached campuses.

Going as a Team

SMs go to the last remaining frontiers in teams to make disciples through incarnation mission.

Up to One Year

SMs serve eight months to one year on the field, planting churches among the unreached.

Training First

One month of training at InterCP International Headquarters. Summer dispatch is June–July; winter, December–January.

Vision School Graduates

SMs are Vision School graduates with six months of campus ministry.

Life After the Field

Back on campus, SMs lead campus ministries and return for short-term church-planting mission trips.
