Where It Meets ---

70 Branches in Korea and 400 Overseas 

Vision School registration is open in the United States and Canada each spring and fall.

There are also more than 4,500 staff serving in 70 branches in Korea and 400 overseas branches through mission education, research, mobilization, and support.

They train 14,000 people world-wide every year as missionary candidates for the frontier unreached people groups.
