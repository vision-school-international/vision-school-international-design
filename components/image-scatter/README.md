# image-scatter

Site component `image-scatter` (CMS block `imageScatter`), defined in `vision-school-international-website-components/src/components/image-scatter/`.

From its stylesheet:

> Statement with scattered photos from the design board ("S44 imgs-sticky"): a 900-tall section with the heading and paragraph centred and five 316×316 photos laid over them. The board is one captured frame of a scroll animation: the heading's words are at opacity 0 and the photos at the positions below. Animating them is left to the website; it can set --vsi-image-scatter-word-opacity or style the words itself.
