What is Youth Vision School?

Through YVS, the students will understand the Father’s heart for all nations and adopt God’s vision as their own to live for the kingdom of God.

01

9 Weeks

YVS is a 9-week missions training program consisting of worship, lectures, prayer, and small groups.

02

Global Mission

YVS aims to educate students about the current global mission condition.

03

God’s Vision

YVS aims to plant God’s vision of global evangelization.

04

The Unreached

YVS aims to equip students to be missionaries in the unreached areas of the world.

05

The Father’s Heart

Through YVS, the students will understand the Father’s heart for all nations.

06

For the Kingdom
