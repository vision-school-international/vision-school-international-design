### 3. Branches need useful class details before registration

Keep the complete directory on its own page. Cards should help visitors recognize the branch, location, and available program/generation. A visitor should then be able to see the class's meeting day, time, venue or sufficiently precise location, and the registration action. Show a public contact only when the team approves that contact route. A branch website can be a separate optional action.

The discussion favors photographs with branch titles and a consistent visual treatment. The choice between branch-specific and suitable fallback imagery still needs content review. Do not make missing photography prevent an otherwise useful layout or misrepresent a stock photo as a real branch.

Adults, Youth, and Kids offerings must be explicit. ANA/YA/YG labels do not reliably establish all available class types. Frederick/DCMD remains one DC/Maryland branch; DCVA is separate. The 37 review entries are not a verified inventory of all active offerings.

**Change:** Strengthen the visitor's class-detail step. An immediate external link alone may not answer the questions that prompted the directory. The team again distinguished private staff contacts and transportation preferences from public information.

**Evidence:** Part 1, 00:27:11–00:32:20, 00:38:22–00:39:24, 00:42:29–00:45:25, and 01:03:51–01:06:07.

### 5. Start designing a custom registration replacement

The discussion moved beyond an unspecified investigation toward registration mockups. The first demonstrated administrative areas were **People** and **Registrations**. Current registrations are separated by Fall/Spring term and United States/Canada, with USD/CAD differences as the reason for keeping country-specific financial handling.

Requested capabilities include:

- Student registration across relevant generations and camps, without disconnected records for each program.
- Clear grouping and filters for program/generation, term, country, and payment status.
- A useful filter for unpaid registrants and a way for authorized staff to send the appropriate payment reminder to the selected group.
- Payment plans as a desired future capability.
- Readable registration dashboards and charts, including branch/program counts and later registration-to-graduation measures.
- A better registration design to guide the subsequent data model.

**Change:** A custom replacement is the intended design direction. It is not authorization to shut down Planning Center, migrate a live payment system, or adopt every capability of that system. The short demonstration did not establish the complete field list, payment/refund rules, permissions, or migration procedure. Keep the working registration service available until a replacement is verified.

The quoted event price in the discussion was an example, not a product price to publish. USD and CAD must not be combined as if their amounts were directly interchangeable. The exact dashboard metrics still need definitions.

**Evidence:** Part 1, 00:31:19–00:38:10; Part 2, 00:00:32–00:05:31 and 00:10:49–00:12:52.

Nation-network information after week four and an application around week five were examples to investigate, not approved hardcoded rules. YGMC was explicitly expanded as **Young Generation Mission Camp**. Other abbreviations and application rules need the responsible ministry owner's confirmation.

**Change:** Replace the former broad "later participant portal" statement with these actual user needs, while keeping them outside the November branch-intake MVP.

**Evidence:** Part 1, 00:00:00–00:03:09 and 00:08:56–00:09:15.

### 7. Preserve a workable transition from CORE spreadsheets

The current CORE spreadsheets remain operationally important. The team discussed both a portal that eventually becomes the editing workspace and a portal that reflects changes made in the sheets. These are alternatives; no final synchronization or cutover design was selected.

The immediate need is clearer, more accessible schedule and travel information. A gradual pilot should establish who edits which record and where the authoritative version lives before two systems are allowed to diverge. Import/export alone must not be described as live two-way synchronization.

**Change:** Do not present the existing CORE prototype as an agreed immediate replacement for the sheets. Anthony recommended protecting the team's current productivity and explained that missed synchronization changes are a real failure case.

**Evidence:** Part 1, 00:48:42–01:01:09.

### 8. Expand travel-entry design, without purchasing an API

Staff want to enter a booking from a phone against the correct speaker and route. They also asked about extracting details from a receipt, itinerary screenshot, or confirmation message. A service-number lookup for flights/trains/buses was discussed as a feasibility question, not a selected paid integration.

Keep manual entry available. Confirmations can become draft fields for a person to verify. Dates, direction, origin/destination, and the selected provider may be necessary to identify a journey; a flight or train number by itself must not be assumed sufficient.

**Change:** Add mobile entry and explicit import options to the existing travel requirements. Choose the source-of-truth/sheet transition before deciding whether an import writes to the portal, a sheet, or a reviewed draft. No live carrier lookup or paid account was approved here.

**Evidence:** Part 1, 00:46:08–00:55:48.
