Young Generation Mission Camp (YGMC)


// INTRO

26 “If anyone comes to me and does not hate his own father and mother and wife and children and brothers and sisters, yes, and even his own life, he cannot be my disciple. 27 Whoever does not bear his own cross and come after me cannot be my disciple. Luke 14:26-27

We are excited to invite you to 2026 Young Generation Mission Camp! The theme of this year's conference is "True Disciples."

Throughout history, God has always anointed young people to advance His kingdom. 100 years ago, 20,500 American students forsook their dreams, sailed overseas as missionaries, and transformed the darkest frontiers. It was the world’s greatest mission movement - Student Volunteer Movement, changing the destiny of thousands of people. God birthed this movement through true disciples who carried their cross.

Today, as the world grows darker and the love of many grows cold, God is raising up true disciples who will love God the most. He is calling our generation to overcome in faith for His final revival! Will YOU go?

May we see our young generation rise up as those who will be faithful unto death. May God pour out His power and grace as we come to worship, pray, and commit together for the final revival and restoration of all nations.

// WHO CAN COME TO YGMC

All 11th-12th graders, College Students and Young Adults (through age 35).
