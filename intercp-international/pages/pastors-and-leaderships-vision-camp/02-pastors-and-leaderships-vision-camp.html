Pastors and Leaderships Vision Camp

As the world now faces increasing uncertainty, chaos, and spiritual darkness, the churches are struggling and decreasing.

The spiritual leadership of pastors must be strengthened in these last days. What if the Churches of World were running without direction and pursuing only local growth? Now is the time to transition the direction to become a mission-oriented Churches. This is God's command. It is the task given to the Churches in our generation.

In these last days, God uses churches to engage in frontier missions. It is time for all ethnic churches of North America to rise to complete the Great Commission in the final frontier. God is sending and using diverse ethnic churches in these times. The ethnic churches of North America, who will move the Global Church and raise leaders, must now rise up with leadership and lead the Global Church strategically and restore the spiritual leadership that will impact the world with God's vision. And in such times as these, we are holding '2023 Pastor's Vision Camp'.

In the name of our Lord, we invite you to 2023 Pastor's Vision Camp.
