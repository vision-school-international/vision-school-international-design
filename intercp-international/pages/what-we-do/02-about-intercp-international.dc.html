About InterCP International

InterCP International is a non-denominational, overseas mission organization, founded in 1983, whose vision is for frontier pioneering mission among the unreached people groups of the 10/40 Window. InterCP International uses creative approach methods to carry out God’s Great Commission in the gospel frontiers and cooperates with all evangelical mission organizations, denominations, and local churches to complete the mission of rapid world evangelization. InterCP International is also a member of Christian Council of Korea (CCK), Korea Association of Tentmaking (KAT) and Kingdom Inter-Missions Network (KIMNET).

As of 2026, InterCP International has commissioned approximately 5,900 missionaries to serve more than 55 people groups in the 10/40 Window, in areas including Asia Minor, the Caucasus, Central Asia, Persia, North India, and the Arab, Siberia-Mongolia, China Frontiers, Volga-Ural, ASEAN, and Maghreb regions. With the central purpose of church planting, we serve these regions through ministries such as education, medicine, area development, research, and volunteer work to carry out a holistic mission approach.  There are also more than 4,500 staff serving in 70 branches in Korea and 400 overseas branches through mission education, research, mobilization, and support to train 14,000 people world-wide every year as missionary candidates for the frontier unreached people groups.

InterCP International is devoted to developing and carrying out innovative tent-making ministries for discipleship and organic church planting. These ministries include student volunteer mission, mercy mission, business mission, social development, education, and other NGO-type ministry.

InterCP International partners with evangelical mission organizations and churches for the overall purpose of global evangelization. We desire to mobilize the global church that will support and be co-ministers through short-term and long-term mission trips, intercession, and financial support to complete the Great Commission. InterCP International offers Vision School and World Mission globally. Vision School is a mission education program that biblically focuses on God’s vision and equips Christians with necessary resources to serve the unreached nations. World Mission is a worship and prayer gathering that raise mission awareness about the unreached nations. InterCP International also organizes annual or semi-annual Mission Camp national conferences that raise mission awareness on a larger scale.
