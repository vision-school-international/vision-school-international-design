CORE VALUES

FRONTIER SPIRIT

Through faith, we overcome difficult and unpredictable situations encountered in the mission field. We live among local people, learn the native language and culture, preach the gospel, and plant churches regardless of circumstance.

Field Spirit

In order to minister in the frontier, creative-approach areas, we recognize the importance of ministry expertise, which comes from spirituality developed through a deep knowledge of the Bible, as well as spiritual leadership, which is acquired through hands-on ministry in a campus or workplace.

Discipleship

While we recognize the effectiveness of ministry through relief, social and cultural exchange, education, business, etc., we believe in following the example of our Lord Jesus, who discipled the twelve and commanded the evangelization of all nations through discipleship. This is the foundation of our ministry strategy and the salvation of souls and planting churches is our ministry principle.

Partnership

In order to see rapid world evangelization, we believe that all evangelical denominations, mission organizations, and local churches must unconditionally cooperate as the body of Christ to raise up financial supporters, intercessors, short-term workers, and long-term workers for the completion of the Great Commission.

Women’s Ministry

We consider the role of women in ministry to be as important and effective as that of men. While there is a need for women-focused ministries domestically, we emphasize the need for women’s ministries overseas. There is a particular need for creative strategies for reaching women in the Islamic World and other frontier areas due to religious and societal restrictions.
