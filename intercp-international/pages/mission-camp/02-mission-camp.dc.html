INVITATION

Greetings in the glorious name of our Lord Jesus Christ! May God fill you and your church with His love and grace for your commitment to the revival and restoration of the nations.

Mission Camp is an annual world missions conference whose purpose is to awaken believers to have radical obedience to our Lord and Savior and to his Great Commission. Through messages, testimonies, worship and intercession, participants will be awakened to live with a global, Great Commission-minded perspective and will learn about the urgency of the movement of the gospel in the context of the times.

Our organization is preparing for the 19th annual Mission Camp for all generations, which will be held at Ontario Convention Center in California this year. Through Mission Camp, we desire to rise together as a holy generation that will be equipped to fulfill God’s promise. There will be separate and specialized programs for children, youth, college students, and adults. Every year we receive countless testimonies from attendees about their radical transformation for the gospel and to live for the Kingdom of God!

We humbly invite you and your congregation to our 2026 Mission Camp. We hope you will consider making Mission Camp a part of your church’s summer retreat/event. If you’re interested in making an announcement at your church, please contact us and our regional InterCP International staff members will be ready and available to make a presentation and/or set up an informational booth at your church.

May you and your church, who are serving diligently for the gospel and the kingdom of God, overflow with God’s abundant grace and peace!

For more information, email us at missioncamp@intercp.org.

RECOMMENDATION

Rev. Mark Schmutz - Northlake Church, Longview, WA

I attended Mission Camp for the first time in 2016. It changed my life. I have been a pastor for over 30 years and taken many mission teams to the nations, but the vision and passion and clarity of calling of the InterCP International movement stunned me! Today I rejoice that the Lord has opened the door to me and my people to go to the nations and fulfill the Great Commission in a powerful way. It was new to us, but it’s the way Jesus sent people out from the beginning. Come and join me at Mission Camp 2024!
