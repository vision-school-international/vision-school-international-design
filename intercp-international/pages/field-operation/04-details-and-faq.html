FO is unlike any other short-term mission trip. You will receive a deeper understanding of who God is and His desire for all tribes, tongues, peoples to return to Him. You will witness the fulfillment of the Great Commission with your own eyes and participate in the fulfillment of God’s history!

The specifics of each FO will depend heavily on the spiritual condition and field realities of each nation.

Field Operation FAQ

Who can go?

ANYONE! Only requirement to participate in FO is completion of Vision School.

What do you do?

For 2-3 weeks, go and share the good news! You will learn the language and culture of the unreached people group you are serving, eating with them, crying with them, praying with them.

When do we go?

Every summer and winter

Where do we go?

To the unreached nations in the 10/40 Window! To places and people who have never had the opportunity to hear the truth of Jesus Christ before.

Why do we go?

To fulfill the Great Commission! “Go, make disciples of all nations...” (Matthew 28:18-20)
