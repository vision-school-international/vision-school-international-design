Field Operation (FO)

For “everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.” How then will they call on him in whom they have not believed? And how are they to believe in him of whom they have never heard? And how are they to hear without someone preaching? And how are they to preach unless they are sent? As it is written, “How beautiful are the feet of those who preach the good news!” (Romans 10:13-15)

God uses His people to fulfill His plans and purposes. The preaching of the gospel to the unreached nations is no different. How can they believe if they have never heard? How can they hear unless someone tells them? In His authority, God has given the task of preaching the gospel to those who have never heard it before to His people, His disciples, His Church.

Each summer and winter, Christians from all around the world step out in faith to take the gospel of the kingdom to the people and the places where the gospel has never been preached before. Trusting in the Lord, guided by the Holy Spirit, they go to places worship upon lands that no Christian has set foot in thousands of years, and preach the gospel where the gospel has never been preached before.
